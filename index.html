Artist's Vision

Art That Lives
On Your Skin

Founded with a singular vision — to elevate tattooing from craft to high art — Ink Boy Tattoo Studio has grown into one of the region's most respected creative studios. Every piece we create is a collaboration between artist and client.

We believe your skin is the most personal canvas. That's why we pour every ounce of creativity, technical skill, and emotional intelligence into each session. From first sketch to final line, you're in the hands of artists who care deeply.

Our studio upholds the strictest hygiene standards. Sterile equipment, single-use needles, and a meticulously clean environment are non-negotiables — because great art deserves a safe foundation.

Meet The Artist

The Hand Behind The Art

INK BOY
Artist & Founder
Sameera Silva "Ink Boy"
Artist & Founder · 7+ Years Experience

Born into a family of visual artists, Alex discovered tattooing at 19 and never looked back. Trained in Colombo and apprenticed under masters in Bangkok and Berlin, he brings a truly global aesthetic to every piece. His work blends technical perfection with raw emotional expression — each tattoo is a portrait of its wearer's soul.

Alex is known for his photorealistic black & grey work, intricate fine-line detail, and the ability to translate even the most abstract concepts into wearable art that surprises and moves people.
